About DEVKI GROUP OF COMPANIES

Learn about East Africa's leading manufacturing conglomerate producing steel, cement, roofing sheets, fertilizers, and industrial packaging.

Who We Are

A Prominent East African Manufacturing Conglomerate

The Devki Group of Companies is a prominent, privately owned manufacturing conglomerate based in Kenya and one of the largest producers of building materials in East Africa.

Devki Steel Mills Limited

Why Choose Us

Why Choose Devki Group of Companies

Choosing the Devki Group of Companies means supporting a leading East African manufacturing conglomerate known for high-quality, locally produced building materials like steel, cement, and roofing sheets at economical costs. The group drives regional industrialization, creates thousands of local jobs, and invests heavily in eco-friendly, energy-efficient manufacturing.

💎

Quality and Affordability

  • Certified standards: Verify the current KEBS mark and permit number on the exact product or packaging supplied.
  • Local production: Items are manufactured locally from raw materials to finished goods, reducing reliance on expensive imports.
  • Competitive pricing: Streamlined production allows for cost-effective pricing for individual builders and large infrastructure projects.
🌿

Sustainability and Innovation

  • Green manufacturing: Environmental initiatives vary by facility; consult current manufacturer and regulatory disclosures.
  • Resource conservation: Confirm current water, waste and environmental-performance information for the relevant facility.
🌍

Community and Economic Impact

  • Rural development: Plants are intentionally built in rural and countryside regions (such as Kwale, Makueni, and West Pokot) to decentralize economic growth.
  • Social upliftment: The group funds local school feeding programs, builds classrooms, and supports community education initiatives.
