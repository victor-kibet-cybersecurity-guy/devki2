Editorial purpose

The technical team prepares distributor-side product explainers, quotation guides, calculator notes and ordering information for customers researching building materials in Kenya.

Verification process

  • Separate manufacturer facts from distributor sales information.
  • Use current product labels, technical data sheets and approved specifications when available.
  • Date price information and remove stale quotations.
  • Correct factual errors when identified.

Source policy

Technical claims should link to authoritative manufacturer documentation, KEBS information, project standards or other primary sources where accessible. The site should not invent certificates, prices, product performance claims or customer reviews.

Update policy

High-change pages such as price guides, stock information and delivery terms should show a visible last-reviewed date.
