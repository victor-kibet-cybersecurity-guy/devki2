Building Materials Budget & Estimation Guide in Kenya

Published: February 2026 • Author: Devki Distributor Quantity Surveying Team • 7 min read

Accurate material takeoff is the foundation of every successful construction project. Below is an approximate benchmark quantity checklist for a standard 3-bedroom bungalow (approx. 120 square meters plinth area) constructed in Kenya:

1. Steel Reinforcement Takeoff (Devki Steel Bars)

  • D12 Columns & Ground Beams: ~50 to 65 pieces (approx. 550 - 700 kg).
  • D10 Slab Matting & Lintels: ~40 to 55 pieces (approx. 300 - 400 kg).
  • D8 Shear Links & Rings: ~60 to 80 pieces (approx. 280 - 380 kg).
  • 16G Binding Wire: 1 x 25kg roll.

2. Cement Takeoff (Simba Cement 32.5R)

  • Foundation Footings & Slab Pour: ~110 to 130 bags.
  • Stone Walling Mortar: ~45 to 60 bags.
  • Internal Plastering & External Rendering: ~50 to 70 bags.
  • Total Cement Estimate: ~210 to 260 bags of 50kg cement.

3. Roofing Sheets Takeoff (Maisha Mabati Box Profile)

  • Roof Pitch & Overhang Area: Approx. 160 square meters total roof area.
  • 3.0-Meter Sheets Needed: Approx. 55 to 62 sheets of 1010mm effective cover width.
