D8 vs D10 vs D12 Steel Bars: Which Rebar Size Do You Need?

Published: February 2026 • Author: Devki Distributor Technical Desk • 6 min read

For most domestic residential developments in Kenya—ranging from 2-bedroom bungalows to 4-bedroom maisonettes—the three most heavily specified rebar sizes are D8 (8mm), D10 (10mm), and D12 (12mm). Each diameter serves a distinct structural function defined by structural engineer blueprints.

1. Devki D8 (8mm): Shear Links, Rings & Stirrups

D8 bars are rarely used as main load-bearing bars. Instead, steel fixers bend D8 bars into square or rectangular rings (stirrups) spaced at 150mm to 200mm intervals along columns and beams. These rings prevent main longitudinal bars from buckling under compressive load and handle diagonal shear stress.

2. Devki D10 (10mm): Suspended Floor Slabs & Staircases

D10 bars provide the main tensile grid reinforcement for suspended concrete floor slabs. Woven into top and bottom mat grids spaced at 150mm or 200mm centers, D10 bars resist sagging bending moments across room spans.

3. Devki D12 (12mm): Primary Residential Columns & Ground Beams

D12 bars act as main vertical rebar inside 225mm x 225mm column pillars and horizontal ground ring beams. They take the direct compressive weight of walls, slabs, and roof trusses above.
