Devki Steel Bars Prices in Kenya: Complete Rebar Buying Guide

Published: February 2026 • Author: Devki Distributor Technical Team • 8 min read

When planning a building construction project in Kenya—whether a single-family bungalow in Kiambu or a commercial apartment block in Kilimani—steel reinforcement bars (rebar) constitute one of the single largest material expenditures. Understanding how Devki steel bars are priced, weighed, and supplied gives developers and contractors significant cost efficiency.

1. How Steel Bars Are Priced in Kenya

In the Kenyan building sector, steel rebar is quoted in two main formats:

  • Price per Metric Tonne (1,000 kg): Used for large commercial sites and bulk hardware purchases.
  • Price per Individual 12-Meter Piece: Used for retail sales and smaller residential sites.

2. Devki Steel Bar Weight Chart (BS 4449 Grade B500B)

Because steel rebar is manufactured to international standards (BS 4449 / KS ISO 6935-2), every millimeter diameter has a standardized linear mass per meter. This allows you to calculate exact tonnage from piece counts:

3. Key Factors Influencing Steel Rebar Prices

Prices fluctuate based on global scrap metal costs, iron ore import tariffs, electric arc furnace power costs, and transport distance from Devki's main steel mills in Athi River and Ruiru to destination towns like Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, or Garissa.
