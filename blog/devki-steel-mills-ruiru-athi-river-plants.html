Inside Devki Group Steel & Cement Manufacturing Plants

Published: February 2026 • Author: Devki Industrial Communications • 6 min read

Devki Group of Companies is East Africa's largest conglomerate manufacturing steel, cement, roofing sheets, and fertilizers. Operating integrated primary industrial plants in Athi River (Machakos), Ruiru (Kiambu), Lukenya, Salgaa (Nakuru), and Samburu (Kwale), the group plays a foundational role in Kenya's industrialization.

1. Athi River & Ruiru Steel Mills

Devki Steel Mills Limited operates electric arc furnace technology and automated high-speed rolling mills capable of producing thousands of tonnes of high-yield BS 4449 Grade B500B rebar and hollow section steel tubes daily.

2. National Cement Clinker & Grinding Plants

National Cement Company Limited operates a multi-billion shilling mega clinker facility in Kajiado/Lukenya along with grinding plants in Salgaa, Nakuru. Producing clinker locally reduces dependence on imported raw materials, stabilizing cement prices for local builders.

3. Maisha Mabati Galvanizing & Color Lines

Maisha Mabati Mills Limited features continuous hot-dip aluminium-zinc alloy coating lines that produce corrosion-resistant AZ150 coils formatted into Box Profile, Corrugated, and Versatile tile roofing sheets.
