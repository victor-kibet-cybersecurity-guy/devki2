How Many Bags of Cement Do You Need for a Concrete Slab?

Published: February 2026 • Author: Devki Distributor Concrete Desk • 6 min read

Pours for suspended floor slabs and ground foundation slabs represent critical concrete volume milestones. Underestimating cement bags halts casting mid-pour, causing dangerous cold joints. Overestimating wastes site budget. Here is the exact calculation method used by site engineers in Kenya.

1. Standard Concrete Mix Ratios in Kenya

  • Class 20 Concrete (1 : 2 : 4): 1 part cement, 2 parts clean sand, 4 parts crushed ballast (20mm). Standard structural mix for ground slabs and residential columns. Requires 6.5 to 7.0 bags of 50kg cement per cubic meter (m³) of wet concrete.
  • Class 25 Concrete (1 : 1.5 : 3): 1 part cement, 1.5 parts sand, 3 parts ballast. Standard mix for suspended floor slabs and beam transfers. Requires 8.0 to 8.5 bags of 50kg cement per cubic meter (m³).

2. Worked Example: 100 Square Meter Slab (150mm Thickness)

  1. Calculate Wet Volume: 100 m² × 0.15 m thickness = 15.0 cubic meters (m³).
  2. Calculate Cement Bags (Class 20): 15 m³ × 7 bags/m³ = 105 bags of 50kg Simba Cement.
  3. Add 5% Site Wastage Factor: 105 × 1.05 = 111 bags total.
