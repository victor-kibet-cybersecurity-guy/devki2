How to Buy Devki Group Products via Official Distributor Channel

Published: February 2026 • Author: Devki Distributor Order Desk • 5 min read

To streamline material procurement for contractors, hardware retailers, and real estate developers, this online platform connects site managers directly with authorized distributor sales desks for Devki Steel Mills, National Cement (Simba), and Maisha Mabati.

Step 1: Submit Your Material Takeoff Requirements

Contact our sales team on WhatsApp (+254 735 361 747) with your structural list (e.g., 2 tonnes D12 rebar, 100 bags 32.5R Simba cement, 60 sheets G28 Box Profile mabati).

Step 2: Receive Official Proforma Quote

Our sales desk generates a competitive proforma invoice detailing product specs, unit rates, VAT breakdown, and site delivery charges based on your county location.

Step 3: Scheduled Site Delivery

Upon payment confirmation, flatbed or crane trucks dispatch your order directly from Athi River, Ruiru, or regional depot warehouses to your construction site.
