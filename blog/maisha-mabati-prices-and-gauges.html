Maisha Mabati Prices & Gauge Selection Guide in Kenya

Published: February 2026 • Author: Devki Distributor Roofing Team • 6 min read

Choosing roofing sheets for your building project in Kenya involves balancing upfront sheet cost, thickness gauge durability, architectural style, and resistance to coastal or high-rainfall weather. This guide breaks down Maisha Mabati products manufactured by Maisha Mabati Mills Limited.

1. Decoding Steel Gauge Numbers: Lower Gauge = Thicker Steel

In steel sheet manufacturing, gauge numbers run counter-intuitively—a lower gauge number signifies a thicker steel core:

  • Gauge 26 (0.40mm nominal thickness): Heavy-duty commercial grade. Maximum wind uplift resistance and longest lifespan.
  • Gauge 28 (0.35mm nominal thickness): The standard premium choice for residential homes across Kenya. Ideal stiffness and durability.
  • Gauge 30 (0.25mm nominal thickness): Light economy grade suitable for budget structures, temporary staff quarters, and perimeter hoarding.
