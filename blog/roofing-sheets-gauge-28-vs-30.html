Roofing Sheets Gauge 28 vs Gauge 30: Which Should You Choose?

Published: February 2026 • Author: Devki Distributor Technical Desk • 6 min read

When selecting Maisha Mabati roofing sheets, understanding the physical performance differences between Gauge 28 (0.35mm thickness) and Gauge 30 (0.25mm thickness) ensures long-term roof stability.

1. Steel Core Thickness & Rigidity

  • Gauge 28 (0.35mm): 40% thicker steel core than Gauge 30. Provides strong resistance against sagging between timber purlins spaced at 900mm to 1000mm centers.
  • Gauge 30 (0.25mm): Lighter sheet requiring tighter purlin spacing (600mm to 750mm) to avoid foot-traffic dents during installation.

2. Thermal & Acoustic Performance

Gauge 28 sheets vibrate less under heavy tropical downpours, noticeably reducing indoor rain noise compared to thinner Gauge 30 sheets.

3. Summary Recommendation

For permanent residential houses, schools, and commercial premises, Gauge 28 Maisha Mabati is strongly recommended for structural longevity. For agricultural sheds or temporary structures, Gauge 30 offers budget economy.
