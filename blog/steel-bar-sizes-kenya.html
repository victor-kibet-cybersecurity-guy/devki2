Steel Bar Sizes & Weight Chart in Kenya (BS 4449 Standard)

Published: February 2026 • Author: Devki Distributor Engineering Desk • 5 min read

In Kenya's structural engineering practice, steel reinforcement bars are designated by the letter D followed by the nominal bar diameter in millimeters (e.g., D12 = 12mm deformed bar). All Devki steel bars conform strictly to BS 4449 Grade B500B and KS ISO 6935-2.

1. Standard Steel Bar Weight Formula

The theoretical mass per meter of a steel bar is calculated using the standard formula:

Mass (kg/m) = D² / 162 (where D is nominal diameter in mm)
