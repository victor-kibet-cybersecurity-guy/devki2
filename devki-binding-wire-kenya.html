Soft Black Annealed Tying Wire

Devki binding wire undergoes a thermal annealing process that renders the steel soft, pliable, and easy to bend around rebar junctions without snapping. Typically, 10kg to 15kg of binding wire is required per metric tonne of steel rebar.

✓Wire Gauge: 16 SWG (1.6mm nominal diameter).
✓Coil Packaging: Standard 25kg bundles.
✓High ductility prevents snapping during hand-twisting with pincer pliers.
