Devki D10 (10mm Diameter) Steel Bar

Devki D10 steel bars offer an optimum ratio of tensile strength and ductility. They are extensively used in top and bottom mat reinforcement for suspended floor slabs, balcony overhangs, and reinforced concrete lintels.

Nominal Diameter:10.0 mm
Linear Weight:0.617 kg / meter
Bar Weight (12m length):7.40 kg per bar
Bars per Tonne (Approx):135 bars per metric tonne
Steel Grade:BS 4449 Grade B500B / KS ISO 6935-2
Primary Uses:Suspended slab matting, staircases, slab beams, lintels
