Devki D8 (8mm Diameter) Steel Bar

Devki D8 steel bars are essential for fabricating shear links and column rings that hold main longitudinal rebars (D12, D16, D20) in position during concrete compaction. They feature distinct ribbing patterns for maximum mechanical anchorage.

Nominal Diameter:8.0 mm
Linear Weight:0.395 kg / meter
Bar Weight (12m length):4.74 kg per bar
Bars per Tonne (Approx):211 bars per metric tonne
Steel Grade:BS 4449 Grade B500B / KS ISO 6935-2
Primary Uses:Stirrups, column ties, rings, ground beams, slab wire mesh
