Ordering process

1. Request quotation

Send the exact product and quantity.

2. Confirm specification

Confirm grade, diameter, profile, gauge or cement class.

3. Confirm destination

Share county, town, landmark and site access.

4. Review quotation

Check material, transport and validity details.

5. Confirm payment instructions

Use only verified official distributor payment details supplied for the order.

6. Dispatch and delivery

Confirm truck, offloading and receiving arrangements.
