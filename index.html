Who We Are

East Africa's Prominent Building Materials Conglomerate

The Devki Group of Companies is a prominent, privately owned manufacturing conglomerate based in Kenya and one of the largest producers of building materials in East Africa. Founded in 1986 by industrialist Narendra Raval, the group began as a small Nairobi hardware shop and has grown into a major conglomerate with over 19 factories, more than 14,000 employees, and an annual turnover exceeding USD 1 billion.
