State-of-the-Art Roofing Innovation

Buyer's Guide

Understanding Gauges & Finishes

Learn the difference between G28 vs G30 gauges and Matte vs Glossy finishes to choose the best option for your budget and building design.

Gauge Guide: G28 vs G30 Thickness

The gauge number indicates sheet thickness (lower gauge number = thicker sheet):

G28 (Gauge 28 - Thicker ~0.32mm)
  • Thicker, stronger, and highly durable
  • Superior resistance to heavy wind and denting
  • Ideal for long-term residential homes and commercial builds
  • Slightly higher investment for maximum durability
G30 (Gauge 30 - Thinner ~0.25mm)
  • Thinner and lighter weight
  • More affordable and cost-effective
  • Great for budget-conscious builds, temporary structures, and fencing
  • May dent more easily under extreme point loads

Summary: G28 offers higher strength and long-term quality, while G30 provides an economical solution for budget-driven roofing.

Finish Guide: Matte vs Glossy

Select the surface finish that matches your aesthetic preference and local solar climate:

1. Matte Finish (Textured Satin)
  • Dull, non-shiny sophisticated surface
  • Modern, premium architectural appearance
  • Reflects UV without harsh glare; highly fade-resistant
  • Scratch-resistant textured coating ideal for hot/sunny areas
2. Glossy Finish (High Shine)
  • Shiny, highly reflective vibrant surface
  • Traditional bright look with smooth finish
  • Slightly more affordable than textured matte
  • Durable coating suitable for all standard residential roofing

Summary: Choose Matte for modern elegance and maximum UV sun resistance, or Glossy for high-shine affordability.
