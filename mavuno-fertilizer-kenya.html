Mavuno Crop-Specific Formulations

Mavuno fertilizers combine Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium (NPK) with essential secondary nutrients (Calcium, Magnesium, Sulphur) and trace elements (Zinc, Boron) to optimize soil pH and root development in Kenyan soils.

✓Mavuno Planting NPK: High Phosphorus blend for strong early root growth.
✓Mavuno Topdressing: High Nitrogen blend for rapid vegetative growth.
✓Specialized formulations for Maize, Potatoes, Wheat, Vegetables, Tea, and Coffee.
