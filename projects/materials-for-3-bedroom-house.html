House-building planning guide

Materials for a 3 Bedroom House in Kenya

Plan the stages, information and material categories needed for a three-bedroom house. Final quantities and costs must come from approved drawings, a measured bill of quantities and current supplier quotations.

Important: There is no safe universal quantity for a “three-bedroom house.” Floor area, storeys, soil, spans, walling system, roof shape, structural design and finishes can change the BOQ substantially.
Stage-by-stage checklist

Core material planning areas

1. Substructure and foundation

Start with the site investigation, setting out and structural foundation design. Typical categories include excavation, blinding, reinforcement, concrete ingredients or ready-mix, foundation walling, damp-proofing, backfill and hardcore.

Use the foundation plan to separate pad footings, strip footings, ground beams and column bases. Do not estimate reinforcement from bedroom count.

2. Ground floor and slab

Measure compacted fill, hardcore, sand blinding, damp-proof membrane, mesh or bar reinforcement, concrete volume and curing requirements from the floor build-up and slab drawings.

Openings, level changes and external aprons should be measured separately.

3. Walling and openings

Calculate net wall area after deducting doors, windows and large openings. Specify stone, block or other walling units, mortar, lintels, damp-proof course and wall ties where required.

Allow for realistic cutting and breakage based on the selected unit and site handling plan.

4. Columns, beams and ring beam

Use structural schedules for bar diameters, quantities, links, laps and concrete cover. Measure formwork and concrete independently from reinforcement.

Never replace an engineer’s bar schedule with a generic online rule of thumb.

5. Roof structure

Confirm whether the design uses timber or steel trusses, then schedule members, connectors, bracing, treatment, fascia and rainwater goods from the roof plan and structural details.

Roof pitch, overhangs, valleys and hips affect both structure and sheet quantities.

6. Roofing sheets and accessories

Specify profile, gauge, finish, effective cover width and ordered lengths. Include ridges, valleys, barge flashings, fasteners, washers, sealants and gutters.

Use effective cover—not nominal sheet width—when planning the number of sheets.

Planning workflow

From drawings to a purchase schedule

Build a measured BOQ

  1. Freeze the architectural and structural drawing revisions.
  2. Measure work by stage and element.
  3. Apply the project specification to every item.
  4. Add transparent waste assumptions only where appropriate.
  5. Separate material, labour, plant, transport and tax.
  6. Have the BOQ reviewed before procurement.

Plan staged purchasing

  1. Order foundation items before superstructure materials.
  2. Confirm secure, dry storage and truck access.
  3. Match product deliveries to the programme and cash flow.
  4. Reconfirm price, stock, specification and lead time for each release.
  5. Inspect labels, quantities and condition before accepting delivery.

Common mistakes

  • Buying from an early drawing that later changes.
  • Treating floor area as a complete cost model.
  • Ignoring laps, cutting, openings, roof complexity and accessories.
  • Mixing units such as pieces, metres, bags and tonnes.
  • Using a cement or steel grade without checking the design specification.
  • Ordering everything at once without storage or security.

Delivery information to provide

  • Site pin and accessible route.
  • Required delivery date and working hours.
  • Truck size or weight restrictions.
  • Offloading responsibility and equipment.
  • Safe storage location and weather protection.
  • Contact person authorized to inspect and sign.

Questions

3-bedroom house material FAQs

How many bags of cement does a 3-bedroom house need?

Bedroom count is not enough to calculate cement. Use measured concrete, mortar, screed and plaster quantities from the drawings and specification, then apply the relevant mix or product design.

How much steel is required?

Only the structural drawings and bar-bending schedule can establish the required diameters, lengths, links, laps and quantities. Use the rebar calculator to check weights after the schedule is prepared.

How do I estimate the cost?

Price a measured BOQ using current material, labour, transport, plant, tax and contingency assumptions. Reconfirm every supplier rate and unit before comparing quotations.

Should I buy all materials at once?

Usually, staged procurement reduces storage, damage and cash-flow risk. The best sequence depends on the programme, supplier lead times, price validity and available secure storage.
