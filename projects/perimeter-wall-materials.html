BOQ and procurement workflow

  1. Confirm the latest architectural, structural and services revisions.
  2. Measure each work item with a clear unit and specification.
  3. Apply transparent waste and lap assumptions only where relevant.
  4. Obtain like-for-like quotations with price validity and tax shown.
  5. Schedule releases to match the programme and available storage.
  6. Inspect quantity, labels, documents and condition at delivery.

Common procurement mistakes

  • Pricing an outdated drawing.
  • Comparing different grades, gauges or units as if equivalent.
  • Omitting accessories, laps, formwork, testing or transport.
  • Assuming a calculator output is a final construction quantity.
  • Accepting an unsupported certification or delivery promise.
  • Ordering before access, storage and offloading are ready.

Planning and delivery checklist

Provide a measured schedule, exact product descriptions, site location, access constraints, preferred delivery stage, offloading responsibility and a contact authorized to inspect the delivery. Reconfirm price, stock and lead time for every release.

Frequently asked questions

Can floor area alone produce an accurate material list?

No. Floor area can start a feasibility check, but final quantities require measured drawings, specifications and a BOQ.

Are online prices final?

No. Confirm the exact unit, specification, quantity, loading point, transport, tax and validity period on a current quotation.

Who should approve structural quantities?

The responsible qualified professional should approve structural drawings, schedules, substitutions and construction quantities.
