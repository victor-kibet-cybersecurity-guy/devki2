Cement Grades

Simba & Rhino Cement Technical Data

Formulated in accordance with KS EAS 18-1 (East African Standard for Portland Cements).

Class 32.5R

Simba Portland Pozzolana Cement (PPC)

Standard: KS EAS 18-1 CEM IV/B-P 32.5R

Initial Setting Time: ≥ 60 minutes

28-Day Compressive Strength: ≥ 32.5 MPa

Primary Applications: General residential masonry, plastering, walling, bricklaying, foundation footings, and domestic floor screeds.

Request 32.5R TDS via WhatsApp
Class 42.5N

Simba Ordinary Portland Cement (OPC)

Standard: KS EAS 18-1 CEM I 42.5N

Initial Setting Time: ≥ 45 minutes

28-Day Compressive Strength: ≥ 42.5 MPa

Primary Applications: Heavy multi-storey structural columns, pre-cast concrete beams, suspended slabs, bridge piers, and high-load civil engineering works.
