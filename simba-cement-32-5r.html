Simba Cement 32.5R Specifications

Simba Cement 32.5R blends fine reactive pozzolana with high-purity clinker to produce a cohesive mortar with enhanced thermal crack resistance and superior resistance to chemical attack in soils.

Grade Classification:CEM IV/B-P 32.5R (KS EAS 18-1)
Bag Size:50 kg net weight
28-Day Strength:≥ 32.5 N/mm² (MPa)
Primary Uses:Brick & block walling, internal/external plaster, domestic foundation slab footings, floor screeds
